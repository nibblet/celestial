## Recording tips

- Use your phone's voice memo app in a small, soft room: a closet or a car works. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly off to the side.
- Read it as if you're telling a friend, not announcing. Pause at the full stops; I'll tighten the gaps in the edit.
- Record each line two or three times in one file, with a breath between takes. I'll pick the best of each.
- Leave a second of silence at the start and end.
